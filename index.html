    How the simulation works

    Crude follows a noisy random walk. The product basket equals 1.21 × crude plus a spread that pulls back toward its mean and sometimes shifts level. Each month the hedge ratio is re-estimated by OLS on the trailing year, so every trade only uses past data. Year 1 is the estimation window.
